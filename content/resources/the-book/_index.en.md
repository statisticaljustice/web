{{< lead >}}
A project extending the core questions of Statistical Justice into a longer-form work on how states classify populations, how official systems produce visibility and exclusion, and what is at stake when people are not recognized on their own terms.
{{< /lead >}}

Drawing on research, conceptual development, and public engagement, the book will bring together many of the themes that shape this project: recognition, redistribution, protection, institutional erasure, and the human consequences of statistical invisibility.

{{< badge >}}The book is currently in development. More information will be shared as the project takes shape.{{< /badge >}}
