This series explores the human stories behind Statistical Justice, the people, absences, and institutional failures that often remain hidden in official data. Through short films, the project seeks to bring together questions of recognition, identity, violence, and public memory in a more intimate and narrative form.

Episode 1: Death Certificates (Teaser)

The first episode begins with memory: a trans woman remembering friends who have died, and the fear of what happens when no one is left to insist, *this is who she was*. 

From there, the film opens onto a deeper question: how identity can be lost, rewritten, or erased in official records after death — and why that loss is not only personal, but institutional.
