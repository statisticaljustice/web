This page brings together podcast episodes and public conversations connected to the Statistical Justice project. The collection approaches statistical justice from different entry points, including legal reform, identity documents, public data systems, institutional practices, and community advocacy. Through conversations with people working across government, research, and civil society, these episodes examine how formal rights become part of government systems, and what is required for recognition to translate into dignity, protection, and meaningful institutional change.

# First Episode


{{< lead >}}
**A historic decree in uncertain times**
{{< /lead >}}

> **Series:** Navegantes
>
> **Organization:** Liga de Salud Trans | **Guest:** Jorge Iván Cuervo | **Date:** 2 August 2026

This episode features a conversation with Jorge Iván Cuervo, former Minister of Justice, about Decree 0732 of 2026 and its significance for the recognition of trans and non-binary people in Colombia. It examines how legal recognition is translated into civil registration and identity documents, the institutional challenges involved in implementing the reform, and the need to protect this advance amid political uncertainty.
