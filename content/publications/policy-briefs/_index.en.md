This page gathers policy-oriented and technical materials that translate research into recommendations, institutional analysis, and practical routes for reform. While the academic papers develop the broader conceptual and empirical arguments, the documents here are more directly focused on policy change, technical design, legal frameworks, and institutional pathways for inclusion.

### Cuando la muerte te destroza: La omisión de las identidades trans en los certificados de defunción en Colombia
{{< badge >}}Policy Brief{{< /badge >}}

This policy brief, based on the published article in the International Journal of Transgender Health, presents the study’s main findings in a more accessible and action-oriented format. It highlights the barriers that trans and non-binary people face in death registration, and calls for changes in the civil registration and vital statistics system.

### Hacia la inclusión de identidades de género diversas en las estadísticas vitales en Colombia
{{< badge >}}Technical Note{{< /badge >}}

This technical review maps the legal, institutional, and policy landscape for including diverse gender identities in Colombia’s vital statistics. It identifies major bottlenecks, recent advances, and possible pathways for reform, including standards, interoperability, legal change, and participatory design.

### El SRCEV Diverso
{{< badge >}}Report{{< /badge >}}

This report situates the Statistical Justice agenda within broader discussions on civil registration and vital statistics in Colombia. It brings together international evidence, legal review, institutional analysis, and reflections on whether Colombia’s vital statistics system is prepared for gender diversity.
